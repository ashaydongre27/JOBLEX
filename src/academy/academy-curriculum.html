Official NEP-2020 summary open_in_new

01 · Multidisciplinary

Flexible programme design

Check for cross-disciplinary electives, minors, interdisciplinary projects, and documented subject combinations.

02 · Skills

Academic and vocational integration

Check whether employability, practical labs, certifications, and skill modules carry academic credit.

03 · Industry

Structured work-based learning

Check for assessed internships, live projects, mentors, measurable MoU outcomes, and evaluation rubrics.

04 · Credits

Portable learning pathways

Check Academic Bank of Credits mapping, transfer rules, re-entry pathways, exit awards, and recognition of prior learning.

05 · Research

Student innovation pathways

Check undergraduate research, funded projects, innovation cells, prototypes, incubation, and external research collaborations.

06 · IKS and outcomes

Responsible knowledge integration

Check scholarly Indian Knowledge Systems content, programme outcomes, course outcomes, assessment rubrics, and attainment evidence.
